## How the roster evolves

Nanitics currently ships with a single maintainer — see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the decision model that matches.

**Additional maintainers** are added when (a) a contributor has landed
sustained, high-quality work across multiple areas over time, (b) the existing
maintainer(s) nominate them, and (c) the nomination is recorded in this file
via PR. The signal is earned track record, not request.

**A maintainer leaves the active roster** by self-removal via PR, or by being
moved to an *Emeritus* list (added to this file when the first such entry
exists) once they step back from active maintenance. Emeritus entries are
retained as an acknowledgement of past contribution; they do not carry current
decision authority.
